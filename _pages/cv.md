# English

## Profile

Mechanical Design Engineer with more than 15 years of experience in designing, manufacturing, assembling and commissioning machinery and industrial plants. Strong at the intersection of mechanical design and practical production, with expertise in SOLIDWORKS, Autodesk Inventor, AutoCAD, CNC machining, G-code programming, technical drawings, tolerances and quality inspection.

## Professional Experience

### CNC Machining / Mechanical Design Engineer
**Getriebetechnik Dessau GmbH, Dessau-Roßlau, Germany** | 2026 - Present

- Set up and operate CNC boring and turning machines for precision machining of mechanical parts.
- Create, adapt and optimize G-code programs on Siemens SINUMERIK 840D/810 and Heidenhain iTNC 530.
- Interpret technical drawings and implement dimensional, tolerance and machining requirements.
- Select tools, apply tool offsets and optimize machining parameters; perform turning, drilling and boring operations.
- Inspect finished parts and troubleshoot CNC programs, tooling and machining processes.

### Mechanical Engineer / Head of Technical Office
**Safa Foulad Co., Iran** | Apr 2017 - 2026

- Designed mechanical components and industrial equipment, including more than 45 belt conveyors and 8 chain conveyors.
- Optimized plant layouts and prepared technical drawings, data sheets, MRQs, MDRs and other engineering documentation.
- Designed dedusting systems, including ducting and cyclones.
- Led and contributed to modernization projects for steelmaking, thin slab caster and hot strip mill facilities.
- Assembled, commissioned and modified mechanical and hydraulic systems.

### Co-Founder / Mechanical Engineer
**Charkh Gardoon Co., Iran** | Jan 2014 - Apr 2017

- Designed a special hydraulic crane for motors and generators up to approximately 40 t.
- Designed mechanical mechanisms and hydraulic systems, reducing installation costs on one project by more than 50%.
- Provided technical leadership for the assembly of approximately 1,000 t of industrial equipment.
- Designed and fabricated an electro-hydraulic grab; inspected manufactured components.

### Mechanical Engineer
**Safa Foulad Co., Iran** | Jan 2010 - Jan 2014

- Installed and commissioned two oxygen plants, each with a capacity of 30,000 Nm³.
- Created a 3D model of the cold-box system with PDMS and led the technical office for cold-box erection and commissioning.
- Commissioned Atlas Copco compressed-air compressors.

## Education

- **M.Sc. Mechanical Engineering**, Yazd University, Iran | Sep 2006 - Apr 2009
- **B.Sc. Mechanical Engineering**, Shahid Chamran University, Ahvaz, Iran | Sep 2001 - Apr 2006

## Technical Skills

- **CAD & design:** SOLIDWORKS, Autodesk Inventor (iLogic, iPart/iAssembly), AutoCAD, AVEVA E3D/PDMS, 3D modelling, assemblies, technical drawings and design for manufacture.
- **CNC & manufacturing:** CNC turning, drilling and boring; G-code; SINUMERIK 840D/810; Heidenhain iTNC 530; tooling offsets, measurement and manufacturing optimization.
- **Mechanical engineering:** machine design, mechanical assemblies, hydraulics, conveying systems, piping, dedusting, cyclones, assembly, alignment and commissioning.
- **Analysis & software:** Python (Pandas, NumPy, scikit-learn), MATLAB, Automation Studio, Belt Analyst, Helix DeltaT.
- **Standards & quality:** DIN, ISO, CEMA, FEM, ASME, tolerances, quality inspection and VT/PT/RT.
